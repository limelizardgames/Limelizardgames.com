Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Lime Lizard Games collects, how that information is used, and the choices available to players who use our website and game services.

Effective Date: September 23, 2026
If we materially update this Privacy Policy, we will post the updated version on this page.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Name, display name, username, email address, and profile information you provide when creating a Lime Lizard Games account.
  • Messages, support requests, and account-deletion requests you send to us.

Information collected automatically

  • Gameplay data such as stats, achievements, badges, match history, and Lime Coin balances.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate diagnostic/log information needed to operate and secure the service.

Social sign-in information

If you sign in through Google or Facebook, we may receive limited information from that provider, such as your email address, name, and profile image, depending on the permissions granted.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage player accounts.
  • To operate Lime Lizard Games titles and connected services.
  • To track game progression, achievements, player profiles, and in-game currency balances.
  • To provide customer support and respond to account or technical issues.
  • To improve site performance, user experience, and future game features.
  • To prevent abuse, fraud, cheating, or unauthorized access.

3. How Information Is Stored and Shared

Lime Lizard Games uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to help operate the platform, including hosting, authentication, analytics, and database services. We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when necessary to operate the service, comply with law, protect our rights, or investigate misuse.

Our authentication and account system may use providers such as Supabase, Google, and Meta/Facebook to support sign-in and account management.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, session storage, and similar technologies to keep players signed in, remember account sessions, improve functionality, and understand site usage. You may be able to control certain cookies through your browser settings, though disabling some technologies may affect how the site works.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain player progress, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce platform policies. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove or anonymize data as described in our data-deletion process, subject to any required retention obligations.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may update certain account details through your Lime Lizard Games account settings.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • You may contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

For data deletion instructions, visit the Data Deletion page.
