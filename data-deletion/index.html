Legal / Account Management

Data Deletion

If you would like Lime Lizard Games to delete your account and associated personal data, use the instructions below.

How to Request Deletion

You may request deletion of your Lime Lizard Games account and associated personal data by using one of the following methods:

  • Email us at support@limelizardgames.com from the email address associated with your account.
  • Include your Lime Lizard username, display name, and the email address tied to the account so we can verify the request.
For security purposes, we may request additional information to confirm account ownership before deleting an account.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Account profile information, including username, display name, and email address.
  • Connected authentication/account records where applicable.
  • Player progression information such as XP, Lime Coins, badges, achievements, and saved stats, unless retained in limited form for security, anti-fraud, or legal compliance reasons.

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited data where necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or security purposes. Any retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Processing Time

We will make reasonable efforts to process verified deletion requests promptly. Actual completion times may vary depending on account complexity and service-provider processing windows.

Questions

If you have questions about data deletion or privacy, contact us at support@limelizardgames.com.
